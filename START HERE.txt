CLAUDE CREW - START HERE
========================

You only need to do ONE thing:

   Double-click the file called  setup.cmd

A setup wizard opens in your browser. It checks Node, git and the Claude CLI,
lets you choose a source (a git clone, never a ZIP), pick the install folder,
set a few options, and opens Crew when it is done.

If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".

You also need a paid Claude account (Pro or Max) to sign in with. Free accounts do not work.
Get one at https://claude.ai

Next time, double-click the "Claude Crew" icon on your Desktop.

To uninstall, double-click uninstall.cmd, or use Settings > General > Uninstall Claude Crew.
A backup of your data is offered by default.

If something goes wrong, the wizard says what to do. You can run setup.cmd again as
many times as you like. It never deletes your settings or chat history unless you
choose Uninstall and confirm.

Full guide: docs\SETUP.md
